# Nagiyev Studio — Homepage Structure

## Role
Homepage = lobby / gateway. Not a showcase of everything. Creates impression of level, shows divisions, explains positioning, routes to the right division. Depth lives inside each contour.

## Sections

### 1. Hero
- Strong headline, short positioning text, **single CTA**
- "AI-powered digital systems, product design and media production for modern organizations."
- Calm, confident, premium
- **CSS animation** — subtle, minimal, premium feel
- No slider — one static hero. Each contour has its own hero on its landing page

### Language Switcher
- **In header, always visible**
- On mobile: outside burger menu, immediately accessible
- EN / AZ toggle

### 2. Core Divisions (3 blocks)
- **Digital Systems & AI** — AI systems, SaaS, dashboards, automation, apps, enterprise workflows
- **Product & Design** — UI systems, interfaces, branding, visual systems, product thinking
- **Media Production** — photography, video, media work, production, creative direction
- Each division = separate visual identity block, mini-site feel within the studio

### 3. Selected Work
- 3–6 curated projects max
- Show breadth: AI, SaaS, UX, branding, media, enterprise
- AzerScope, Logitaca, AI systems, dashboards, UI systems, selected media

### 4. Studio Philosophy / Approach
- Short block explaining why studio combines software + AI + design + media
- Core message: builds full digital ecosystems, not just websites or content

### 5. Clients / Collaborations
- Trust block: logos, organizations, holdings, community partnerships, institutions
- Creates sense of real experience even without famous brands

### 6. About Preview
- Studio lead, multidisciplinary background, philosophy
- Link to full About page

### 7. Final CTA / Contact
- Clean, calm, non-aggressive
- "Discuss a project", "Start a collaboration", "Contact the studio"
- WhatsApp, Telegram, phone links (no form)

## Vibe
- Expensive, calm, confident, systematic, modular, enterprise-friendly

## Avoid
- Chaotic gallery, "I can do everything", long bios, freelancer style, Behance wall, text overload

## Model
- **Homepage**: creates level + routes
- **Divisions**: sell competency
- **Cases**: prove experience
