Media is not just content for filling space. Good photography, video and sound shape how people perceive a business, city, product, event or organization.

This direction is built around high-quality visual and audio production that can be used across websites, presentations, books, exhibitions, social media, catalogues, campaigns and public communication. The strongest results come when media is planned as part of a larger brand, design or digital system.

## Photography

Photography is one of the strongest parts of this direction. The experience covers many types of professional photography, including event photography, international and institutional event coverage, city and urban photography, parks, boulevards and public spaces, architecture and large urban structures, interior and exterior photography, hospitality and hotel photography, product photography, cultural photography, scientific photography, astrophotography, industrial photography, production process photography, and photography for websites, catalogues, books, exhibitions and public campaigns.

## City, Architecture & Hospitality

This direction includes strong experience in city, architecture, interior and exterior photography. Relevant work includes city commissions, public spaces, architectural structures, hotels, hospitality complexes, boutique hotels, interiors, exteriors, commercial property visuals, and visual content for travel, booking and presentation platforms. This type of work is especially relevant for hotels, developers, tourism projects, city projects, public institutions and businesses that need strong visual representation of physical spaces.

## Product & Brand Visuals

Product and brand photography is part of business communication. This includes product visuals, premium and rare product photography, catalogue photography, campaign visuals, website visuals, presentation materials, social media content, and visual assets for brand communication. The goal is not only to show the product, but to present it with the right level of quality, context and trust.

## Scientific & Astrophotography

Scientific and astronomy-related visuals are a distinctive part of this direction. This includes professional astrophotography, scientific visual materials, astronomy exhibitions, educational science visuals, visual content for lectures, seminars and public education, and public science communication. This capability connects media production with education, science, design and public communication. It is especially useful for projects where visual material must explain, inspire or make complex subjects accessible to a broad audience.

## Industrial Photography

The experience also includes industrial photography for production environments. This covers industrial facilities, manufacturing processes, production lines, technical environments, process documentation, corporate reporting visuals, management-level presentation materials, and investment and business communication materials. This is useful for companies that need serious visual material for reports, websites, catalogues, investor communication or internal documentation.

## Video Production

Video production is handled through the studio's production network and can include concept, shooting, editing, visual direction, interview formats, promotional content and event coverage. Relevant work includes event video content, international exhibitions and conference coverage, interviews, promotional videos, educational videos, scientific visual sequences, video content for websites and social media, and campaign and presentation video materials.

## Sound, Music & Audio

Sound is a major strength of this direction. This is not an add-on to video production — it is a serious professional background that can support brand communication, media content, campaigns, events and digital products. Capabilities include sound design, music composition, corporate soundtracks, jingles, intro and identity audio, audio editing, voice and dialogue cleanup, mixing, mastering, music production, recording studio experience, and recording label experience. Nagiyev Studio can approach media not only visually, but also sonically.

## Creative Direction

This direction also includes creative direction: concept, idea development, scenario, visual style, photography direction, video direction, content structure, editing supervision, sound direction, and packaging for websites, social media, presentations and campaigns. The studio can think through the whole media package, not only produce isolated assets.

## Content for Websites, Social Media & Presentations

Media production connects directly with the other two directions of the studio. Photography, video and sound can become part of websites, landing pages, product pages, brand presentations, pitch decks, annual reports, social media campaigns, event campaigns, scientific and educational materials, public communication, and corporate catalogues. The value is stronger when media is produced as part of a larger brand, design or digital system.
