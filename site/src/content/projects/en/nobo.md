NoBo was designed as a compact CRM system for organizations that work with members, documents, payments and recurring administrative processes.

Instead of keeping operations across spreadsheets, chats and manual folders, NoBo brings key NGO workflows into one structured digital environment.

The current version focuses on clean mechanics: document flow, operational automation, payment accounting and report preparation. AI assistance is planned as a future layer for prediction, guidance and faster administrative work.

NoBo is currently built around reliable mechanics rather than AI. This makes the system practical from day one, while leaving room for intelligent assistance later.
