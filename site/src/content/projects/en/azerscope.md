Nagiyev Studio built AzerScope as a practical astronomy platform, not just a visual sky guide.

The product helps users understand when to observe, what conditions matter, how sky events work and how to stay connected with astronomy-related activity.

The platform brings together several layers: observation planning, astronomy tools, scientific data, educational explanations, multilingual content, community profiles and participation logic.

The goal is to turn scattered astronomy information into a clean, usable and mobile-first experience.
