## Who We Are
Nagiyev Studio is a multidisciplinary digital practice based in Baku, Azerbaijan. Led by Faik Nagiyev, the studio operates at the intersection of technology, design, and media.

## Our Philosophy
We do not believe in isolated deliverables. A beautiful design is useless without robust engineering, and a powerful system fails if users can't navigate it. We build cohesive ecosystems where every part serves the whole.
