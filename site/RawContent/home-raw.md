# Home Page Content — Nagiyev Studio

## Page Role

The home page should work as a calm, concise entry point into Nagiyev Studio.

It should not explain every service in depth. Its purpose is to quickly show that the studio works across three main directions and guide the visitor toward the relevant section or the contact page.

The tone should be solid, clear and professional. No hype, no exaggerated promises, no aggressive marketing language.

---

## Main Positioning

Nagiyev Studio creates digital systems, visual communication and media content for local businesses, organizations and public projects.

The studio helps clients build a complete digital presence — from structure and strategy to design, websites, content and operational tools.

Do not use fixed URLs here. The editor/agent should connect each card to the correct route according to the project structure.

## Philosophy / Approach

Nagiyev Studio works at the intersection of technology, design and media.

We do not treat a website, a logo, a presentation or a video as isolated tasks. For most businesses, these elements are part of one larger system: how the organization presents itself, communicates with people and operates digitally.

Our work is focused on creating clear, complete and practical digital packages — not disconnected fragments.

## Notes for Editor / Implementation Agent

The home page should stay concise.

Do not overload it with full service descriptions. Each of the three main directions should have only enough text to help the visitor understand the scope and click deeper.

Avoid presenting internal products as clients. Use “Selected Work” or similar wording unless the section contains only actual client names.

Avoid leading with “AI-powered” in the hero. AI can be mentioned inside the Digital Systems direction, but the main positioning should remain broader and more stable.

The website should feel like a serious local studio with broad multidisciplinary capability, not like a generic AI agency.
