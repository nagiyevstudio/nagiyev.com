# Digital Systems — Raw Content

## Page Role

This page should present the Digital Systems direction of Nagiyev Studio.

The page should not sound like a generic software development agency page. It should explain that this direction is based on long practical experience in building websites, portals, internal tools, business logic, automation, CRM systems, fintech-related applications, public-sector software, production reporting systems and AI-assisted workflows.

The tone should be serious, grounded and professional. No hype, no exaggerated AI language, no vague “we build everything” positioning.

---

## Main Positioning

Digital Systems is about building practical digital infrastructure for businesses, organizations and public projects.

This work is not limited to writing code or creating websites. In many cases, it includes understanding the client’s real processes, designing business logic, structuring workflows, consulting, digitizing manual operations and building the software layer around those needs.

The goal is to create systems that are useful, maintainable and connected to how the organization actually works.

## Experience Background

The experience behind this direction goes back to 1999 and includes work across different generations of the web and business software.

This includes personal websites and blogs, corporate websites, large Azerbaijani portals, online commerce systems, social and community features, forums, advertising platforms, B2B catalogues and multi-service web platforms.

The background also includes software and websites for public-sector organizations, including government committees and ministries.

Another important part of the experience comes from industrial digitalization: production accounting, technical progress tracking, quality control reporting and management-level reporting systems for major metallurgical enterprises in Azerbaijan.

The work was not only technical. It also included business logic, process analysis, consulting and operational structuring.

## Industrial and Business Systems

Nagiyev Studio’s Digital Systems direction is informed by experience in complex operational environments.

This includes digital systems for large production enterprises, where software was used to track production, quality, quantity, technical progress and reporting. Such systems were used by senior management to monitor real business and production indicators.

The experience also includes standardization work connected to ISO 9001 quality management and laboratory processes.

This background helps the studio approach digital projects not only as interface or coding tasks, but as systems connected to real operational control.

## CRM and Process Logic

The experience includes building business logic for CRM and educational management systems, including work connected to education-sector processes.

This is important because many organizations do not only need a website. They need structured flows: requests, users, records, statuses, communication, reports, approvals and internal control.

Digital Systems can therefore include CRM-like logic, admin panels, internal workflows and structured operational tools.

## When This Is a Good Fit

This direction is suitable for clients who need:

- a serious website or digital presentation;
- a platform instead of a simple static website;
- a system for managing users, content, requests or records;
- automation of repeated internal processes;
- digitization of paper-based workflows;
- internal dashboards or reporting;
- AI-assisted workflows;
- a practical technical partner who can think through business logic, not only write code.

## What Not to Promise

Avoid promising everything.

Do not present the studio as a bank-core developer, cybersecurity company, SAP/Oracle integrator or high-load enterprise infrastructure provider.

Complex integrations, high-load systems, regulated fintech, banking-grade infrastructure and enterprise ERP-level projects should be presented only as custom-scoped work after analysis.

The page should communicate strong experience, but stay grounded and realistic.
